🐙 Fact 1: Octopus

Octopuses have three hearts, nine brains, and blue blood. Two hearts pump blood to the gills, while the third pumps it to the rest of the body.

How smart are octopuses?

Octopuses can open jars, solve puzzles, and even recognise human faces. Scientists consider them one of the most intelligent invertebrates on Earth.

🦑 Fact 2: Earth's Speed

Earth travels around the sun at a speed of about 67,000 miles per hour.

Fact 3: The Great Barrier Reef

The Great Barrier Reef is the world's largest coral reef system, stretching over 2,300 kilometers.

Fact 4: Honey Never Spoils

Archaeologists have found pots of honey in ancient Egyptian tombs that are over 3,000 years old and still perfectly edible.

Fact 5: Bananas Are Berries

Bananas are actually berries, as are strawberries, blueberries, and tomatoes.

Fact 6: Water Can Boil and Freeze at the Same Time

Under certain conditions, water can boil and freeze simultaneously. This phenomenon is known as the "triple point," where temperature and pressure are just right for all three phases (solid, liquid, and gas) to coexist in equilibrium.

Fact 7: The Shortest War in History

The shortest war in history was between Britain and Zanzibar on August 27, 1896. Zanzibar surrendered after just 38 minutes.

h2>Fact 8: The Longest Word in English

The longest word in the English language is "pneumonoultramicroscopicsilicovolcanoconiosis," a type of lung disease caused by inhaling very fine silica dust.

Fact 9: The Eiffel Tower Can Be 15 cm Taller During the Summer

Due to thermal expansion, the Eiffel Tower can grow up to 15 centimeters taller during the summer when the iron expands in the heat.

Fact 10: A Day on Venus is Longer Than a Year on Venus

Venus has an extremely slow rotation, taking about 243 Earth days to complete one rotation on its axis. However, it only takes about 225 Earth days for Venus to complete one orbit around the Sun, making a day on Venus longer than a year on Venus.
