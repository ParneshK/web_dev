Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam

The Missile Man of India

Early Life and Education

Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam was born on 15 October 1931 in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu, India. He worked hard as a student and developed a strong interest in science.

Achievements and Contributions

Kalam contributed to India's space and missile programmes. He was known for his determination and love for science. He believed that hard work and learning are important. His life is a great inspiration for students. He served as the 11th President of India from 2002 to 2007.

Lessons from His Life

Kalam showed that anyone can work towards their dreams. Success comes without hard work. The correct idea is that success requires effort and dedication.

Kalam encouraged students to dream big and work hard.

Dr. Kalam believed that young people could build a better future through knowledge, courage, kindness and determination. His life teaches us to stay curious, learn from mistakes and keep working towards our goals.
